# OUC-Haide WEAVE education framework

Source: supplied edu-wiki说明.pdf, pages 2–3. Attribution: OUC-Haide, 2026. This is a summary of the team's education framework and reported refinements. The official education route is https://2026.igem.wiki/ouc-haide/education.

WEAVE describes the full education design, implementation, evaluation and improvement cycle:

- **Wonder — Why do we design this activity?** Start from project context and any available feedback. Identify the intended audience, the reason for choosing it, its educational needs, questions and knowledge gaps. State unknown needs as assumptions to confirm.
- **Explore — How can we design for meaningful learning?** Define clear learning goals, then choose scientific content, communication methods and interactive formats suited to the audience.
- **Action — How do we bring the design to life?** Translate the objectives into practical online or offline delivery. A classroom timetable belongs within this stage.
- **Verify — What did participants gain?** Use participant feedback and other evaluation evidence to examine whether the intended objectives were achieved and what participants gained.
- **Echo — What can we improve next?** Collect feedback, reflect on strengths and limitations of content and activity design, and translate those findings into improvements. Implement and evaluate the changes in later iterations.

The supplied source describes three refinement examples: visual concept cards before a laboratory escape-room activity; a short explanation paired with clay modeling; and laboratory videos and images showing experimental procedures, challenges and solutions. These are attributed descriptions from the source, not outcomes produced by this software example.

For a new activity, keep needs and feedback that have not been supplied explicitly unknown. Specify evidence to collect under Verify and conditional revisions under Echo. Do not claim a delivered lesson, measured gains or completed improvements without the corresponding implementation and evaluation evidence.
