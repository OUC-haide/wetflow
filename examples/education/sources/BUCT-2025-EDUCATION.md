# BUCT 2025: BUCT: drawing-to-clay learning with individual support

Source: https://2025.igem.wiki/buct/education
Location: Equ-Globalizing: children with special educational needs
Checked: 2026-10-26. Attribution: BUCT iGEM 2025; source Wiki CC BY 4.0. Paraphrased summary.
Resource status: reported_activity. Category: inclusive-tactile.

Reported practice: BUCT used illustrated explanations, drawings and colored-clay models, with individual facilitator support.

Reported reflection or evidence limit: Teacher reflections and participant creations were used as feedback about participation and understanding.

New adaptation suggestion (WetFlow, not a source outcome): Offer drawing and tactile options; collect a learner explanation and a facilitator observation rather than relying on speed or a written quiz.
