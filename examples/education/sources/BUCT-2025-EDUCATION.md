# BUCT 2025: tactile and age-adapted education

Source: https://2025.igem.wiki/buct/education
Location: Equ-Globalizing — activities for children with special educational needs.
Attribution: BUCT iGEM 2025; original page CC BY 4.0. This is a paraphrased source summary.

BUCT describes a two-day activity moving from illustrated explanations and drawings to colored-clay models of cells or engineered bacteria. Facilitators supported children individually and helped them translate drawings into models. The team collected teacher reflections and participant work as feedback. A shorter workshop could retain the drawing-to-model sequence, offer more than one way to respond, and ask facilitators to record where support was needed. Those changes are new planning suggestions, rather than outcomes reported by BUCT.
