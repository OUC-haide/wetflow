# Joint outreach proposal: source summary

Source: https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6147/wiki/education/6-3-1-milestone-01-ouc-haide-initial-ideas.pdf
Location: joint activity design suggestions, route-puzzle and green-manufacturing workshop sections.
Status: a design proposal. Suggestions and intended evaluation outputs are not completed event results.

The proposal connects the team's straw-to-sugars-to-fermentation-to-PMA-to-material route with the partner's plant-to-product route. A classroom card puzzle asks learners to arrange each route, compare the steps, then choose how to use a new plant waste. Other proposed formats include a stakeholder hearing, a microbe job-matching game and a four-station escape challenge. The proposal recommends beginning with raw material, transformation and product rather than a long explanation of gene regulation. Suggested evaluation outputs include route explanations, decision reasons, questions, hints used and feedback for later revisions. It also outlines a 60-minute joint workshop.
