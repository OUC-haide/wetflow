# OUC-Haide activity refinement: pre-test and equipment

Source: supplied edu-wiki说明.pdf, page 12. Attribution: OUC-Haide, 2026. This summarizes the team's reported activity design history; it is separate from any new guidance produced by this software example.

The initial design asked learners to distinguish simulated samples labeled β-16 and β-17 by comparing reporter fluorescence with reference records. The source reports that a pre-test at Qingdao Academy did not produce a sufficiently clear and controllable fluorescence distinction for the intended reasoning task.

After discussing the available laboratory equipment with Qingdao Academy, the team redesigned the task around digital pH meters. The revised design asks learners to measure the samples and compare their results with reference ranges for β-16 and β-17, retaining the evidence-comparison step while using the available equipment. The source provides no sample recipes or numerical reference ranges here; those inputs must be supplied and pre-tested before reproducing the laboratory activity.

For a classroom adaptation, first check that learners can distinguish the supplied evidence and that the necessary equipment is available. A room without measurement instruments can use prepared evidence cards for the reasoning task. That substitution is a proposed adaptation, not a completed classroom result. Record observations, reflect on the practical limitation, and use the findings to choose and evaluate a later revision.
