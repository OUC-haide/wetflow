# Team activity refinement

Source: supplied edu-wiki说明.pdf, page 12. This describes the team's activity design history; it is separate from any new guidance produced in this demonstration.

Card 01
In our initial design, players would distinguish β-16 from β-17 by comparing their reporter signals.
They would tested prepared simulated samples, measure their fluorescence intensity, and
compare the results with experimental records to determine the true identity of the displayed
sample.
Card 02
Before implementation, the activity lead from Qingdao Academy tested the experimental
component of our initial design to evaluate whether it could reliably produce the intended efficacy.
During this test, we found that the fluorescence-based method could not generate a sufficiently
clear and controllable distinction between the simulated samples, making it difficult to reliably
support the intended storyline progression.
Card 03
Therefore, we redesigned the activity to enhance the audience’s experimental experience. During
our discussion with the Qingdao Academy team, they pointed out that their laboratory was
equipped with digital pH meters, providing us with a feasible alternative detection method. Based
on this feedback, we replaced the original fluorescence-based identification with pH-based
measurement. In the refined design, participants would use digital pH meters to measure the
samples and compare their results with the reference ranges of β-16 and β-17, allowing them to
follow the same evidence-based reasoning process while making the experiment more accessible
and practical.
