# SOP: shaken-flask fermentation (synthetic)

status: draft  applicable_organisms: [Aureobasidium sp. SYN-A08]

## Parameters
- TEMP: CPP, target 25 C, lower 20 C, upper 30 C. Out-of-window readings must be recorded as a deviation.
- PH: PROCESS, target 5.5, lower 4.5, upper 6.5.
- DO: PROCESS, target 30 %, lower 10 %, upper 60 %.

## Order of operations
Seed preparation must be completed before fermentation starts. A run may not move from seed
preparation directly to analysis; every stage transition needs a named approval.

## Failure branches
- contamination_detected: invalidate the batch.
- reading outside a parameter window: open a deviation and keep the raw value.
